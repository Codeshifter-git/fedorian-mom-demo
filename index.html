How to use this planner

Start on Today. The planner opens on today — set your Top 3 Focus and let the rest be optional.

Capture, don't organise. Anything that pops into your head goes in the Brain Dump. Sort it later, only if you feel like it.

Missed a day? Nothing happened. There are no streaks to break and nothing turns red. Just open today and begin again.

Low-energy days are planned for. The Today tab has a short Bare Minimum list for low-spoon days — the bare minimum still counts.

Everything saves itself. Type, tap, close the tab. It's all here when you come back.

One planner for the whole life. Health tracks sleep, movement and a meds log; Home holds chores and maintenance; Kids, School and Meals hold the family side. Each area is a simple list or log — nothing here grades you.
